01. THEORY / OVERVIEW

Algorithm Description

Booth's algorithm is an efficient method for multiplying signed binary numbers in 2's complement representation. It examines the least significant multiplier bit and an additional previous bit to determine the required arithmetic operation.


Decision Logic

00

HOLD / NO OPERATION

01

ADD M TO A

10

SUBTRACT M FROM A

11

HOLD / NO OPERATION


After the selected operation, an arithmetic right shift is performed on the combined A, Q and Q-1 registers. The process continues until the iteration counter reaches zero.
